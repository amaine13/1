About Judy

Student. Clinician. Teacher. Author.

Judy Mahler Steinfeld, LCSW, has spent more than four decades in the therapy room. The bus framework came from watching the same patterns surface in different people — and finding language that made those patterns workable.

Columbia Clinical training
Gestalt Center Faculty, New York
40+ years In the room
Three cities NY · LA · Santiago

How she works

Judy trained at Columbia University and completed postgraduate work at the Gestalt Center for Psychotherapy and Training in New York City. Gestalt therapy — present-moment awareness, the empty chair, the whole picture — became the foundation of everything she does. She served on the Gestalt Center’s faculty through the 1980s.

Her approach is experiential rather than analytical. She is less interested in helping people understand their patterns from the outside than in helping them encounter those patterns directly.

The question is not what is wrong with you. It is what happened to you.

Teaching & speaking

Judy has presented the framework to Rotary Clubs, the American Women’s Club, business groups, writers’ groups, and book clubs across New York, Los Angeles, and Santiago. The most common question afterward: when will the book be available?

The work on the page

Who’s Driving Your Bus? is the first book in a broader body of work. Future volumes will take the same passengers into intimate relationships, aging, and loss.
