Work With Judy

Wherever she presents the framework, the response is immediate: people recognize themselves, and they want more.

Judy works with individuals, groups, book clubs, professional circles, and community organizations across New York, Los Angeles, and Santiago, Chile — bringing The Bus Framework to anyone ready to look inward with honesty and compassion.

The most common question after every session is always the same: when will the book be available?

Whether you have finished the book and want to explore your own passenger roster, or you are a therapist or coach looking to integrate the framework into your practice, these conversations are built around you.

Reading Who's Driving Your Bus? together opens a different kind of conversation — one that moves quickly from the page into the room. Who This Is For

You do not have to be in crisis to do this work.

The people Judy works with are not broken. They are emotionally literate, often self-aware, and frequently frustrated that awareness alone has not been enough to change things. They have likely read other books, been in therapy, or both. What they have not yet found is a framework that makes their inner world feel navigable rather than overwhelming.

The Bus Framework meets people where they are. Whether you are in the middle of a difficult transition, working through a pattern that keeps repeating, or simply ready to understand yourself more honestly — the work begins wherever you are willing to look.
