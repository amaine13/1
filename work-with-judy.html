Work With Judy

People recognize themselves. Then they want more.

Individuals, groups, book clubs, and organizations across New York, Los Angeles, and Santiago, Chile. Write Judy to inquire — she will tell you what is possible right now.

Individual sessions

For women, men, and couples navigating a pattern that will not break, a relationship that keeps repeating, or a quiet sense that something is off. Honesty, warmth, no judgment.

Workshops & retreats

Guided reflection, group conversation, and Bus Stop Practices. Participants do not just learn about their passengers. They meet them.

Group sessions

One person works with Judy while the room witnesses. Vicarious therapy: each person receives the work, even when they are not the one in the chair. No analysis. No suggestions. Real things can finally be said.

Book clubs & organizations

Guided conversations for reading groups, clinical teams, leadership circles, schools, and community organizations. You have ridden a bus. So have the people you work with.

Who This Is For

You do not have to be in crisis to do this work.

The people Judy works with are often self-aware and frustrated that awareness alone has not been enough. The Bus Framework meets them where they are.
