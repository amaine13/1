Work With Judy

Wherever she presents the framework, the response is immediate: people recognize themselves, and they want more.

Judy works with individuals, groups, book clubs, professional circles, and community organizations across New York, Los Angeles, and Santiago, Chile — bringing The Bus Framework to anyone ready to look inward with honesty and compassion.

The most common question after every session is always the same: when will the book be available?

Individual Sessions

Judy offers individual sessions for women, men, and couples. Whether you are navigating a pattern you cannot seem to break, a relationship that keeps repeating itself, or simply a quiet sense that something is off — these sessions are designed to meet you exactly where you are, with honesty, warmth, and no judgment.

Workshops & Retreats

Judy leads workshops and retreats that bring The Bus Framework to life through guided reflection, group conversation, and Bus Stop Practices. Drawing on her training at the Gestalt Center, these sessions go beyond lecture — they are experiential. Participants do not just learn about their passengers. They meet them.

Group Sessions

Judy's group sessions are unlike anything most people have experienced. One person works at a time with Judy while the rest of the group witnesses — and what unfolds is something remarkable: vicarious therapy. Each person in the room receives the work, even when they are not the one in the chair.

There is no judgment, no analysis, no suggestions made. What emerges instead is a group that bonds on a deeper level of shared intimacy — a safe, non-confrontational space where real things can finally be said. It is a powerful experience that stays with people long after the session ends.

Professional & Organizational

Therapists, coaches, educators, and organizational leaders have found The Bus Framework to be an accessible, memorable tool for helping clients and teams understand inner patterns. You do not need to know what a "part" is. You have ridden a bus. You already understand — and so will the people you work with.

Judy offers professional training and organizational conversations tailored to your context, whether that is a clinical team, a leadership circle, a school, or a community organization.

Who This Is For

You do not have to be in crisis to do this work.

The people Judy works with are not broken. They are emotionally literate, often self-aware, and frequently frustrated that awareness alone has not been enough to change things. They have likely read other books, been in therapy, or both. What they have not yet found is a framework that makes their inner world feel navigable rather than overwhelming.

The Bus Framework meets people where they are. Whether you are in the middle of a difficult transition, working through a pattern that keeps repeating, or simply ready to understand yourself more honestly — the work begins wherever you are willing to look.
