Work With Judy

Connect With Judy

Judy works with individuals, groups, book clubs, professional circles, and community organizations across New York, Los Angeles, and Santiago, Chile — bringing The Bus Framework to anyone ready to look inward with honesty and compassion.

A chance to look at familiar patterns from a different perspective.

Individual Sessions

Judy offers individual sessions for women, men, and couples. Whether you are navigating a pattern you cannot seem to break, a relationship that keeps repeating itself, or simply a quiet sense that something is off — these sessions are designed to meet you exactly where you are, with honesty, warmth, and no judgment.

Workshops & Retreats

Judy leads workshops and retreats that bring The Bus Framework to life through guided reflection, group conversation, and Bus Stop Practices. Drawing on her training at the Gestalt Center, these sessions go beyond lecture — they are experiential. Participants do not just learn about their passengers. They meet them.

Group Sessions

Judy facilitates group experiences that invite listening, reflection, and greater awareness. Participants may recognize something of themselves as they hear others speak. Contact Judy to discuss group formats and availability.

Professional & Organizational

The bus metaphor offers therapists, coaches, educators, and other helping professionals a way to discuss familiar responses and patterns. Judy can explore how these ideas may be used for reflection and dialogue in professional settings. You do not need to know what a "part" is. You have ridden a bus. You already understand — and so will the people you work with.

Judy offers professional training and organizational conversations tailored to your context, whether that is a clinical team, a leadership circle, a school, or a community organization.
