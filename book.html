The Book

Who’s Driving Your Bus?

A Practical Guide to Loving Yourself—For Real

Twenty-five chapters. Twenty-one named passengers. A bus you already know how to ride.

About the book

Most people have been told to love themselves so many times that the phrase has stopped meaning anything. They know they should. They just do not know how. The reason self-love feels so elusive is that we keep trying to override the parts of ourselves that are afraid, rather than understanding them.

You do not need to know what a “part” is. You have ridden a bus. You already understand.

A line from the book

“Don’t worry. There’s always another bus.”

The first three chapters — Meet Your Bus!, How Passengers Board the Bus, and Emotional Baggage — are for Early Readers. The full sample stays behind the account gate.

Bus Stop Practices

Every chapter closes with a pause — notice what is happening inside, name it, and bring the framework into real life. These are invitations to listen, not exercises to perform perfectly.

For book clubs

When people work through these questions together, passengers get named out loud. Judy is available to join reading groups for guided conversations. Write her to inquire.
