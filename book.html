The Book

Who's Driving Your Bus?

A Practical Resource for Personal Growth and Therapeutic Work

A book about noticing the different parts of ourselves, learning to see from another perspective, and meeting our inner passengers with compassion.

Read the Introduction

Stories That Feel Familiar

Judy draws on her own life and decades of work with people to explore why we react as we do. You may recognize a familiar feeling, a relationship pattern, or a passenger you never thought to name.

What You Will Explore

The chapters introduce different passengers, explore where they came from, and invite you to notice what changes when you view a situation from another seat.

Bus Stop Practices

At the end of each chapter, a Bus Stop Practice offers a chance to pause and try the ideas for yourself. Some ask you to sit with a feeling. Others invite a conversation with a passenger or a closer look at a familiar reaction.

Inside the Book

Explore the Chapters

A look at the questions, stories, and practices you'll find inside Who's Driving Your Bus? Select any chapter to read a short preview.

Getting to Know Your Passengers

01Meet Your Bus!

Judy begins with a childhood memory and introduces the bus, its passengers, and the question of who has the wheel.

02How Passengers Board the Bus

How early experiences, family patterns, and the need to feel safe can shape the passengers we carry into adult life.

03Emotional Baggage

What our passengers carry, from unexpressed feelings to shame, and how that emotional weight can influence our choices.

04The Passenger Roster

Meet the different passengers and learn to recognize their energy, the masks they wear, and what may lie underneath.

05Why They All Want to Drive

Why even difficult passengers believe they are protecting us, and why they resist giving up control.

06A New Way Forward

An introduction to meeting frightened passengers with compassion, including the empty-chair exercise and the practice of pausing before reacting.

Awareness, Connection, and Change

07Showing Up and Becoming the Witness

How to become a compassionate witness to your own experience, with attention, presence, and less judgment.

08Going Deeper into the Nervous System

A closer look at how we avoid difficult feelings, how the body responds, and what it can mean to stay present with an emotion.

09Metaphors, Imagery, and Dreams

Using metaphors, images, and dreams to give form to feelings that can be hard to express in words.

10Hug Your Demons or They’ll Bite You in the Ass

Why fighting the parts we dislike can keep us stuck, and how acceptance can make room for understanding and integration.

11When Staying Hurts

Exploring the discomfort of staying with difficult feelings, and the difference between struggling through change and rushing to fix it.

12Emerging from the Cocoon into the World with Vulnerability and Truth

What it takes to show our more vulnerable selves, speak honestly, and become visible in our relationships.

13When Two Buses Meet

Judy draws on her own relationship experiences to explore what happens when two people bring their different passengers together.

14Victim Consciousness

Recognizing the Victim passenger, acknowledging real harm, and exploring how to reclaim a sense of choice.

15Giving Your Eyes Away

How we lose sight of ourselves when we imagine what others think of us, and how to return to our own perspective.

16When the Student Is Ready, the Teacher Appears

Finding lessons in difficult experiences, recognizing teachers in unexpected places, and learning from role models.

17We Mock the Things We Are to Be

How words, reactions, and the atmosphere we create can reflect our defenses, and what changes when we meet them with curiosity.

Living With Greater Awareness

18Unity of the Passengers

How passengers can stop competing for control and begin contributing their strengths to a more integrated whole.

19Nature vs. Nurture

Exploring our true nature beneath learned defenses and the ways childhood experiences can shape our sense of self.

20What Are You Doing With One Foot in the Past and One Foot in the Future?

Why dwelling on the past or worrying about the future can keep us from the experience of the present moment.

21The Courage to Surrender and Trust

The difference between surrender and giving up, with practical reflections on control, trust, and letting go.

22Moving Forward

Taking the compassion practiced within ourselves into our relationships and learning how to be present with someone else's pain.

23Living Your Truth

What it means to be honest about who we are today, without performing for approval or hiding behind old roles.

24Joy as a Way of Being

Finding joy through gratitude, appreciation, and a more accepting relationship with the whole of our experience.

25From Lost to Finding Me

Judy brings the bus back to its starting point and reflects on what changes when we recognize our passengers and begin trusting our inner direction.

Chapter titles and summaries are based on the current v13 manuscript. They may be refined as Judy completes her edits.
